01 / About

A little
background.

I’m a researcher working on the connections that keep people, devices, and aerial systems in sync.

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Electrical Engineering & Computer Science at the University of Kansas, where I joined the Wireless Communication and Networked Systems (WCNS) group in 2022. My work brings together wireless communications, distributed optimization, and control for UAV networks.

I earned my B.Sc. in Telecommunication Engineering, with a minor in Computer Engineering, from K. N. Toosi University of Technology in 2020. My undergraduate research explored restoration methods in optical fiber networks. During my Ph.D., I earned an M.Sc. with Honors in 2025, focusing on distributed optimization and control algorithms for UAV networks.

Beyond research

Volleyball, video games, and billiards at a professional level.

02 / Research

Connections, by design.

From the wireless channel to the learning algorithm, I explore how networks can work better together.

01

UAV communications ↗

Transmission control · Interference · Queuing

Personalization · D2D networks · IoT

04 / Experience

Ideas into
practice.

Research, simulation,
and hands-on teaching.

University of Kansas2022 — Present

Graduate Research Assistant

Developing analytical models and optimization methods for wireless and networked systems.

Research contributions +
  • UAV networks, with USRA. Developed a theoretical framework for throughput and PSNR in unlicensed spectrum, optimizing cross-layer parameters for UAV-to-ground and aerial links.
  • mmWave networks. Built a simulation framework for blockage scenarios, capturing blocker characteristics and transmitter–receiver geometry.
  • Satellite networks. Implemented single- and multi-beam communication models to analyze outage probability across GEO–LEO satellite and terrestrial links.
  • Personalized federated learning. Developed a channel-aware neighbor selection policy based on transmission error.
  • RIS-assisted NOMA. Analyzed BS-to-UAV communications to study the interplay between power allocation and reflecting elements.
